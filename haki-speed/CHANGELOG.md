# Changelog

## 1.4.0

- Restored the wheel modifier with a Disabled option; recorded hotkeys take priority.
- Middle mouse button can be recorded as a hotkey combination.
- Clicking the speed digits on the player bar silently resets to 1×.
- Step size options from 0.05× to 1.00×.
- Ambient effect reworked into slow liquid ripples spreading from the pointer.

## 1.3.0

- Universal hotkey recording for keys, wheel directions and mouse buttons.
- Ambient colour drift and pointer fog, with an on/off switch.
- One-time migration of older settings.

## 1.2.0

- One controller per document, with all videos on a page controlled together.
- Hover bar with idle timeout, draggable floating panel and fullscreen support.

## 1.0.0

- First release: floating playback speed panel with per-site memory.
