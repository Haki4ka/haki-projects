Appearance

The floating panel is hidden by default. Show it with the toolbar button or its hotkey. The small player bar appears only while the pointer is over a video.

Speed behavior

The wheel modifier is a quick shortcut over a video or either panel. Disabled turns it off completely. Any combination recorded in Hotkeys still works and takes priority.

Hotkeys

By default Shift + Wheel Up is faster and Shift + Wheel Down is slower. Click any row and then press a key, scroll the wheel, or click any mouse button — left, middle (wheel click), right, back or forward — with any combination of Ctrl / Alt / Shift / Meta. Esc cancels. Wheel and mouse-button combos work over a video or either panel; key combos work wherever the page has focus. A recorded combo wins over the Wheel modifier. Browser-level shortcuts can be rebound in brave://extensions/shortcuts.
