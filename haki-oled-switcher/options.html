Haki OLED — Options

checking…

When off, websites are left as they are. This does not affect the black browser theme.

Main controls are in the compact menu: click the H button on the browser toolbar — there are two independent switches (browser theme and site darkening).
This page is a secondary option. You can also right-click a page → Haki OLED: disable/enable site darkening, or press Alt+Shift+D. That hotkey now turns both themes on together, and pressing it again turns both off. You can change the shortcut in brave://extensions/shortcuts.
